# Riziki system — how to use it

For the shop, not for a computer person. Keep this near the counter.

Everything runs in the phone's web browser. There is nothing to install. Open it
once, tap **Add to home screen**, and after that it opens like any other app.

---

## Signing in

Pick your name, type your PIN, done.

**Everyone must use their own name.** Every sale, every price you drop, every
change to stock is saved against whoever was signed in. If two people share one
PIN, none of that means anything, and a shortage cannot be traced to a day or a
person.

**Change the starting PINs before you open.** The system ships with Owner 1234
and Attendant 1111. Anyone who has seen the system knows them. Go to
**Users & settings** and change both. The screen will nag you in red until you do.

---

## Selling — the counter screen

Tap **Sell**.

1. Tap the item. Tap it again to add another.
2. Tap the green bar at the bottom (**Pay KES …**).
3. Tap how they paid.
4. Tap **Complete sale**.

Four taps. There is no "are you sure" — the queue does not wait.

**Finding things.** The most-sold items sit at the top. Otherwise type in the
search box: it knows the other names too, so *sles* finds Ungerol and
*soda ash* finds Magadi.

**Wholesale prices.** Tap **Wholesale** and every price changes at once. The
whole screen turns orange so nobody can miss it, and it goes back to retail by
itself after each sale.

**Half cash, half M-Pesa.** On the payment screen, type an amount and tap **Cash**,
then type the rest and tap **M-Pesa**. Keep going until the bill is covered.

**M-Pesa.** You must type the code from the message. The system will not accept
the same code twice, so nobody can pay for two things with one message. Wait for
the message on the *shop's* phone — a customer's screen can be faked.

**Giving a discount.** Tap the price on the line and type the lower one. You can
come down to the shop's floor price on your own. Below that the owner has to type
their PIN, and the system writes down who allowed it.

**Selling on credit.** On the payment screen choose the customer, then pay in
less than the total. What is left becomes their debt. If they are already over
their limit the system warns you — it will still let you sell, because you know
your customers, but it says so first.

---

## When there is no network

Keep selling. Nothing changes.

If the network is down, the sale is kept **on the phone** and the screen tells
you so plainly. The pill at the top says **Offline** and how many sales are
waiting. When the network comes back they send themselves. You do not have to do
anything, and nothing is counted twice.

Stock numbers may be a little out of date while you are offline. The screen says
so under the products.

**Only selling works offline.** Mixing, stock takes, buying and reports need the
network.

---

## Breaking a drum into packs (Repack)

Tap **More → Repack**.

1. Choose the drum or bag you are opening.
2. Say how many you are breaking down.
3. Type how many packs of each size you filled.

Watch the **In / Out / Loss** box. If you opened a 170 kg drum and packed
167.5 kg, it shows 2.5 kg lost and what that cost you. That is spillage and
scoop residue. It has always happened; now you can see it.

The system will not let you save a repack that packed out more than went in, or
one that uses a drum you do not have.

---

## Mixing a batch

Tap **Batch**.

1. Choose the product and type how many litres you want.
2. The system works out every chemical and checks the shelf.
3. Tap **Run batch**. The chemicals come off stock.
4. When the mix is done, **type how many litres you actually got**.

That last step matters. If you aimed for 100 litres and got 97, type 97. The
cost is spread over what you really made, so the price per litre is true and you
can see how much every batch loses.

Each batch gets a number like `RZK-20260803-001`. Put it on the label — it is
how you trace a complaint back to a mix.

**Attendants** can mix without seeing the recipe. They get the batch number and
whether there is enough on the shelf, nothing more.

---

## Debts

Tap **Debts**.

Who owes you, biggest first, oldest debt flagged in red once it passes 30 days.

- **Record payment** — put in what they paid. It clears the oldest bill first.
- **Remind on WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp with the amount already typed.

Balances are worked out from the sales themselves, so they cannot drift.

---

## Buying stock

Tap **More → Suppliers & purchases**. Put in what came, from whom, and what it
cost. Add the transport if there was any.

This is what keeps your prices honest: when a drum costs more, the system works
out the new average and your profit figures follow it.

---

## Closing the day

Tap **More → Day close**, every evening.

It shows what should be in the drawer: your float, plus cash taken, minus cash
you paid out. Count the drawer and type what you found.

If it does not match, the difference is saved. One odd night is nothing. The same
shortage on the same person's shifts, week after week, is something — and you can
only see it because it was written down every day.

M-Pesa and credit are left out. Neither is money you can count tonight.

---

## Reports — owner only

Tap **Reports**.

- What you sold, what it cost, what you paid out, what is left.
- Profit on each product, using the price you really charged.
- Repacked chemicals against mixed products — which side of the business earns.
- Chemicals sitting unsold, and what is tied up in them.
- Losses from repacking and stock takes.

Attendants cannot open this, and cannot see cost prices anywhere.

---

## Things worth knowing

**A mistake on a sale.** Do not try to delete it. Open **More → Sales history**,
find it, and void it with a reason. The sale stays on the record with a line
through it and the stock comes back. Then ring it up properly. A system where
sales can vanish is a system where money can vanish.

**Somebody leaves.** Go to **Users & settings** and switch them off. They are
signed out of the counter phone immediately.

**Getting your data out.** Reports → the download links. Everything comes out as
a spreadsheet, whenever you want, and it is yours.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The screen will not load** — check the network. If you were mid-sale, it was
  saved on the phone.
- **"That M-Pesa code has already been used"** — the code was typed on an
  earlier sale. Check the message again.
- **A PIN is forgotten** — the owner can set a new one in Users & settings
  without knowing the old one.
- **Stock looks wrong** — do a stock take (More → Stock take). Count the shelf
  and enter what is there; the system records the difference instead of quietly
  correcting itself.

Anything else, call your developer.
