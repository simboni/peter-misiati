RIZIKI POS

Everything the system does

A counter, stock, mixing, credit and profit system for a chemicals shop — one database, one server, no monthly seat fees.

Selling

  • One board of every chemical and product, colour-coded, searched by name or street name
  • Sell by the kilo, the litre, the piece or the pack — priced per unit, no mental arithmetic
  • Haggling within limits: a floor price nobody goes under without the owner's PIN, and a ceiling that catches a fat finger
  • Split tenders — part cash, part M-Pesa, on one bill
  • M-Pesa codes are unique in the database, so one SMS can never pay twice
  • Wholesale and retail price tiers per customer
  • Works offline. The till keeps selling with no network and syncs when the line returns
  • Void a sale with a reason; the stock and the money both come back

Stock

  • Append-only ledger: nothing is edited or deleted, corrections are further entries
  • Held in thousandths of a unit, so 250 g and 0.25 kg are the same number
  • Repack a drum into jerricans, with the real loss recorded
  • Container counts — how many 20 L jerricans stand filled
  • Reorder levels, low-stock and below-zero warnings
  • Stock take with variance, and shrinkage month by month
  • Dead stock: what has not moved in 60 days and what it cost
  • Weighted-average cost, kept per unit, not per drum

Recipes and mixing

  • Versioned formulas with mixing steps, scaled to any batch size
  • Mixed to order (billed at the counter) or mixed in advance (batched onto the shelf)
  • A batch takes the ingredients off the shelf and puts the product on it, in one transaction
  • Cost follows the yield — water carries mass, not money
  • Record what actually came out, not what the recipe hoped for
  • Undo a batch; the formula never prints on a customer's receipt

Customers and credit

  • Credit limits per customer and debtor ageing by the day
  • Statements, and reminders ready to send on WhatsApp
  • Quotes that become invoices, and invoices that become delivery notes
  • Delivery note with a signature block and a title-retention line

Money and the day

  • Day close: cash counted against what the system expected, with the variance flagged
  • Expenses by category, on the day they were paid
  • Price history — every change, who made it, and when
  • Discounts: what was given away, by whom, and on what

The owner's dashboard

  • Right now: today's takings, today's profit, what is owed, what the shelf is worth
  • Any period against the same number of days before it, with the shape drawn under each figure
  • A computed watch list: what is wrong, what it costs, and the screen that fixes it
  • Sales and profit charted daily, weekly or monthly
  • Money in by the day it arrived — cash against M-Pesa, credit given, old bills settled
  • What earns, per product and per line of business
  • Day-by-day ledger, the whole book, dead stock, shrinkage
  • Sections fold away and remember what you left open
  • Export to PDF, a spreadsheet per table, or the whole database

Who sees what

  • Two roles — owner and attendant — plus six switches: costs, recipes, products, purchases, stock take, voids
  • Cost and profit never reach an attendant's phone: the server refuses before it answers
  • PIN sign-in per person; an activity log of everything anyone did

Papers and printing

  • Thermal receipts over Bluetooth (ESC/POS) from the counter phone
  • A5 invoices and delivery notes on ordinary paper
  • Any document shared straight into WhatsApp as a file
  • What prints on a receipt is set by the owner and stays set

Running it

  • Self-hosted on a small server — no per-seat fee, and the data stays yours
  • Installs onto the phone's home screen like an app
  • One command deploys; an overnight update runs only when there is something to deploy
  • Nightly off-site backup, a restore script, and a health check

What it is made of

One Next.js application and one SQLite database on a small Linux server, behind HTTPS. No cloud account to lose, no per-user licence, no data leaving the country. The whole shop fits in a file small enough to e-mail, and a backup of it goes off the server every night.

Every figure in the reports is recomputed from the raw tables in the test suite — sales, cost, expenses, the chart bars, cash against M-Pesa — and checked to the cent before a release goes near the counter.
