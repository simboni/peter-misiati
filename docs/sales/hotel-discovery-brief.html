How we’d build it — in stages

You don’t have to decide the whole system now. Each stage stands on its own and pays for itself before the next one starts. Tick where you’d like to begin.
STAGE 1  Be found & be trusted
A professional hotel website — rooms, photos, location, contact, WhatsApp enquiries and an enquiry form. Fast, mobile-first, and built so booking can be switched on later without a rebuild.
Start here
STAGE 2  Take direct bookings
Live availability, online booking and payment (M-Pesa and/or card), automatic confirmation emails, and a simple reservations calendar for reception. This is the stage that starts cutting OTA commission.
Include this
STAGE 3  Run the front desk
Property management: room diary, check-in / check-out, guest register and history, folio & guest billing, receipts and invoices, housekeeping room status, and daily reports for the owner.
Include this
STAGE 4  Restaurant, bar & stores
Point of sale and stock: table & room-charge orders, kitchen/bar tickets, printed receipts, stock control for kitchen, bar and housekeeping, supplier purchases, wastage and stocktakes.
Include this
STAGE 5  Control & grow
The owner’s layer: revenue & occupancy dashboards you can see from anywhere, staff roles and permissions, an activity log of who did what, corporate accounts & credit, KRA / eTIMS-ready invoicing, channel sync with Booking.com, and multi-property support.
Include this
