# The 14-Day "Go Loud" Launch Sprint

You have the site, the case studies, the CRM, the scripts, the opportunity map and now the ad creatives. This is the two-week push to convert all of it into conversations. **~1 hour/day.** Track every reply in `smp-crm.html`.

## Rules of the sprint
- **Lead with the deadline niches.** "SHA 90-day HMIS" and "eTIMS 2026" ads convert far harder than "I build websites" — urgency + relevance beats features. Run those two loudest.
- **Every post/ad ends in one action:** WhatsApp +254 706 289 514 or smp-developers.com.
- **Reply within the hour.** Speed wins in the Kenyan SME/clinic market.
- **Log everything** in the CRM. If it's not in the pipeline, it didn't happen.

## Days 1–2 — Set the stage (do once)
- [ ] Post the **flagship StackUp** launch + the **new capability card** on LinkedIn + WhatsApp Status.
- [ ] Create/finish the **Facebook Page** + link **Instagram business** + **Google Business Profile** ("Software developer · Nairobi/Bungoma"). These are your ad rails and free search presence.
- [ ] Load all 8 month-1 posts into **Meta Business Suite** scheduler; schedule LinkedIn posts (⏰) for the fortnight.
- [ ] Add your **5 happiest past clients** to the CRM and fire the reactivation script. (This alone usually lands the first deal.)

## Days 3–10 — Go loud (daily)
Each day, pick a niche and do all three:
1. **Post one ad** to WhatsApp Status + one channel (rotate: clinic → eTIMS → websites → StackUp → SACCO → hospitality → repeat).
2. **Send 5 direct messages** to lookalike prospects for that niche (scripts in `outreach-templates.md`; CRM auto-fills + logs).
3. **Engage 5 prospects' posts** (comment with value, not a pitch) so your name shows up before you DM.

Suggested daily niche rotation:
| Day | Loud on | Outreach target |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Clinic HMIS ad | private/faith clinics, dispensaries |
| 4 | eTIMS ad | dukas, SMEs, accountants (referral partners) |
| 5 | Websites ad | gyms, schools, law/accounting firms |
| 6 | StackUp flagship | founders, teams, tech community |
| 7 | SACCO pitch | tier-2/3 SACCOs, chamas |
| 8 | Hospitality (Zuri proof) | hotels, lodges, Airbnbs (Nyanza/Western) |
| 9 | Political (Wamalwa proof) | aspirants, MCAs, public figures |
| 10 | Best-performing ad, boosted | the niche that replied most |

## Days 11–14 — Convert + amplify
- [ ] **Follow up everyone** who went quiet (follow-up #1 and #2 scripts). Most yeses come here.
- [ ] Turn any **discovery call** into a proposal same day (proposal script).
- [ ] Ask every recent client for a **review** (review script) → publish on the site + GBP.
- [ ] Review CRM: **which niche and which channel produced the most replies?** Double the budget/effort there next fortnight.

## Optional paid boost (only if you have KES 500–1,000/day)
Start **after** day 3, one campaign, the winning niche:
- **Clinic HMIS** → objective: WhatsApp messages · audience: Kenya, clinic/health-admin interests · creative: the 90-day ad.
- **eTIMS** → objective: WhatsApp messages · audience: Nairobi/Eldoret/Bungoma small-business owners · creative: the "taxable" ad.
- Kill anything over ~KES 300/conversation after a week; pour budget into the winner. (Full targeting in `advertising-playbook.md`.)

## The only 4 numbers that matter this sprint
1. Ads/posts shipped (target: 8+) · 2. Outreach sent (target: 40+) · 3. Replies (target: 8+) · 4. Calls booked (target: 4+).
Hit those and 1–2 signed projects follow. Tell me the numbers each Friday and I'll write the next fortnight.
