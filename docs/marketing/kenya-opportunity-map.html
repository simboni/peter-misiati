Where Kenya's software gaps have a deadline attached

Six unsolved problems across government, health, finance, education and agriculture — scored on how well they fit what SMP already builds (payments, claims, records) and how open the door is right now. Ranked by a composite of portfolio fit, gap size, regulatory urgency and how crowded the field is.

The strategic read

Regulation is manufacturing urgent demand in exactly the areas SMP is strong — payments, claims and records.

Three of Kenya's largest software gaps now carry government deadlines: SHA's 90-day mandate for every hospital and clinic to run a digital HMIS or lose accreditation, KRA's January 2026 rule cross-validating every business expense against an eTIMS invoice, and the ICRMS 2025 regulations requiring all 47 counties to run an integrated revenue system. You don't have to invent a market — you have to be the affordable, local, M‑Pesa‑native option for the long tail that Kwara, big HMIS vendors and county ERP incumbents skip.

The gap

KRA's own eTIMS-Lite (USSD/eCitizen) is clunky; nobody owns the friendly, WhatsApp-simple layer on top.
