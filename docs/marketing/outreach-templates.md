# Outreach Scripts — copy, personalise the [brackets], send

**Rules that make these work**
- Personalise the first line — prove you looked at *their* organisation.
- One clear ask: a 15-minute call. Never "let me know if interested".
- Follow up twice: day 3 (gentle) and day 8 (close the loop). Most yeses come from follow-up #1.
- Always attach/link the case study that matches their world.

---

## 1. Past-client reactivation (start with all 20+)

> Hi [Name], Peter from SMP Developers here — hope [organisation] is doing well!
> I've just rebuilt my portfolio and [their project] is featured as a case study: [link]
> Two quick things:
> 1) If you were happy with the work, a one-line Google review would mean a lot: [GBP link]
> 2) I have capacity for new projects — if you know an organisation that needs a website or system, I'd genuinely appreciate an intro. I say thank you with 10% of any signed project.
> How has [their site/system] been performing?

## 2. NGO (lead with COSDEP / Canossian Sisters)

> Hello [Name], I came across [NGO]'s work on [specific programme] — impressive impact in [county/area].
> I'm Peter, a Nairobi-based software engineer. I recently built the online homes for COSDEP Kenya (sustainable agriculture) and the Canossian Sisters' East Africa mission — sites built to turn donors and partners into supporters: smp-developers.com/work/cosdep-kenya
> A strong web presence is often the difference between a proposal that gets funded and one that doesn't. Do you have 15 minutes this week for a quick call about what that could look like for [NGO]?

## 3. SACCO / microfinance / chama (lead with systems experience — HIGH VALUE)

> Hello [Name], I'm Peter Misiati — a software engineer with 10+ years building financial systems in Kenya, including microfinance systems, SACCO platforms and TallyPay (invoicing + M-Pesa billing): smp-developers.com/work/tallypay
> Many SACCOs are still running on spreadsheets or systems members can't access from their phones. I build member portals, loan tracking and M-Pesa-integrated systems — built here, supported here.
> Would a 15-minute call this week make sense to see what [SACCO] members currently struggle with?

## 4. SME (gym / school / firm — lead with the matching case study)

> Hi [Name], I noticed [business] on [Google/Instagram/passed by in town] — [genuine one-line compliment].
> I'm Peter, I build fast, modern websites for Kenyan businesses — recent work includes Fit Generations Gym (Bungoma) and Misiati & Associates (CPA firm): smp-developers.com/work

## 6. Follow-up #1 (day 3)

> Hi [Name], following up on my note about [topic]. I know inboxes are busy!
> Short version: I build [websites/systems] for [their sector] — here's one I shipped recently: [case link]. 15 minutes this week?

## 8. Review ask (send 3–7 days after any launch)

> Hi [Name], so glad [project] is live! 🎉 If you have 2 minutes, a short review would help me a lot — it's how organisations like yours find me:
> [Google review link]

## 9. The proposal follow-up (after any call)

> Hi [Name], great speaking today. As promised, here's the summary:
> **What you need:** [1–2 lines]
> **What I'll build:** [3 bullets max]
> **Timeline:** [X weeks] · **Investment:** KES [amount] ([50%] to start, [50%] at launch)
> **Next step:** reply "let's go" and I'll send the agreement + invoice today. I can start [date].
