# SMP Developers — 90-Day Marketing Plan

**Goal:** a steady pipeline of client work — target **2–3 signed projects per month** by day 90.
**Positioning (one line, use it everywhere):** *"I build the software your organisation runs on — websites, systems and fintech, shipped end to end."*

---

## 1. Where your clients actually come from

Look at your own portfolio — the evidence is already there:

| Niche | Proof you have | Deal size | Sales cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NGO / mission websites** | COSDEP, Canossian Sisters, Talitha Kum, KeYSA | Medium | Short — decision by 1–2 people |
| **SME / business websites** | Fit Generations, Misiati & Associates, Zuri Hotels | Small–medium | Short |
| **Political / public-figure brands** | Dr. Dennis Wamalwa, Misiati MC | Medium | Short, referral-driven |
| **Schools / e-learning** | Golden Star, Holy Cross, e-Learners Academy | Medium | Medium |
| **Fintech / systems (SACCO, ERP, billing)** | TallyPay, Naveedex, 64 Theatre, microfinance work | **Large** | Long — needs trust |

**Strategy in one sentence:** use the *fast* niches (NGO, SME, political, schools) for monthly cash-flow and referrals, while the *systems/fintech* track builds slowly toward bigger contracts.

## 2. The three channels that matter (skip everything else)

### Channel 1 — Referrals & past clients (highest ROI, start here)
Your 20+ past clients are your sales team. Every happy client knows 3 organisations like theirs.
- Message every past client (reactivation script in `outreach-templates.md`): check in, ask for a Google/LinkedIn review, and ask *"who else should I be talking to?"*
- Offer a **10% referral thank-you** on any signed project.
- After every launch: ask for the review + referral within 7 days, while excitement is high.

### Channel 2 — WhatsApp + LinkedIn presence (your shop window)
In the Kenyan market, WhatsApp Status and LinkedIn are where SME/NGO decision-makers actually see you.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2–3 posts/week (the capability cards we made, project launches, before/after shots). Zero cost, seen by everyone who has your number.
- **LinkedIn:** 2 posts/week (calendar with ready drafts in `content-calendar.md`). Every project launch = a post. Every post links to the case study on smp-developers.com.
- Consistency beats brilliance: same day/time each week.

### Channel 3 — Direct outreach (fills the gaps)
10 targeted messages per week to organisations that *look like your past clients*:
- NGOs in Kenya with outdated/no websites (search NGO directories, county CSR lists).
- SACCOs and microfinance groups (your systems background is rare and valuable here).
- Gyms, schools, law/accounting firms in Nairobi, Eldoret, Bungoma — you have proof for each.
- Scripts per niche in `outreach-templates.md`. Always lead with the *relevant* case study.

## 4. Weekly operating rhythm (≈4 hours/week)

| Day | Task (30–60 min) |
|---|---|
| Mon | Post #1 (LinkedIn + WhatsApp Status) |
| Tue | 5 outreach messages (one niche per week) |
| Wed | Follow up everyone from last week (silence ≠ no; 2 follow-ups minimum) |
| Thu | Post #2 + engage: comment on 5 prospects' posts |
| Fri | 5 outreach messages + update `leads.csv` + tell me the numbers |

**My part (tell me each Friday):** who replied, who went quiet, what's stuck — I'll draft the follow-ups, proposals, and next week's posts. Send me any new project screenshots and I'll turn them into launch posts + case studies + updated marketing cards.

## 5. Measure only these

- Outreach sent / replies (aim: 10/wk sent, ≥2 replies)
- Discovery calls booked (aim: 2/wk by day 30)
- Proposals sent → signed (aim: 1 signed per 3 proposals)
- Where each signed client came from (double down there)

## 6. 90-day milestones

- **Day 30:** LinkedIn + GBP live, 5 real reviews collected, 40 outreach sent, first 2 calls.
- **Day 60:** 1–2 signed projects, first referral deal, 8 posts published, case study for every new launch.
- **Day 90:** 2–3 projects/month rhythm, pick the winning niche and go deeper (e.g. "the SACCO systems guy" or "the NGO web guy of Kenya").
