Profile

Most of what I build moves money or records that somebody depends on: M-Pesa collections and receipting, double-entry ledgers, school fee accounts, microfinance and ERP tooling. I design for the conditions the software actually meets — intermittent connectivity, shared servers, staff on a phone rather than a laptop — and I ship end to end, from schema and API through deployment, monitoring and the runbook the operator reads at one in the morning. What I would bring to this role

  • Mobile money in production, in Kenya. M-Pesa Daraja STK Push collection with automatic receipting, per-vendor credentials encrypted at rest, and Kopo Kopo subscription billing — the same rails GiveDirectly uses to reach recipients.
  • Correctness-first money handling. Integer KES cents, VAT in basis points, double-entry ledgers recording every transaction for reconciliation, with the arithmetic unit-tested rather than assumed.

  • Deployment that cannot take the neighbours down. My most recent release joined a new service to a server already running a live point-of-sale and nineteen other containers: config validated inside the running proxy, applied by graceful reload, every existing site probed before and after, automatic rollback if any of them regressed.
  • Security by construction. Fail-closed PostgreSQL Row-Level Security with the application role set NOBYPASSRLS, two-stage scoped JWTs, Argon2id, append-only audit logs — and a CI gate that asserts cross-tenant isolation on every push, so the guarantee is tested rather than documented.
  • Proximity to the people served. My clients are a rural school in Bungoma County, a chemicals trader, community organisations and NGOs. I have spent ten years building for users on cheap Android phones and patchy data, which is the environment, not an edge case.
